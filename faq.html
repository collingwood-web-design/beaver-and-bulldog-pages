Frequently Asked Questions

Everything guests ask about the best wings in Collingwood, our hours, patio, kids menu, and visiting from Blue Mountains, Wasaga Beach, and nearby towns. Still need a hand? Call 705-446-9711.

Wings & the pub

Who has the best wings in Collingwood?

Beaver & Bulldog is Collingwood’s sports pub and wingery, known for world-famous fresh jumbo buffalo wings with 25 flavours cooked in 100% trans fat free oil. Guests across South Georgian Bay come for our wings, beer, and big-screen sports atmosphere.

How many wing flavours do you serve?

We serve 25 flavours of wings. Ask about the Flavour of the Month when you visit, and see the full wing list on our menu.

What is Tuesday Wing Day at Beaver & Bulldog?

On Tuesdays, jumbo wings are $1.45 each for dine-in only with the purchase of a beverage. Wing Day pricing is not available for takeout.

Is Beaver & Bulldog a sports bar?

Yes. We are an innovative sports pub and wingery with lots of TVs for live games, a full pub menu, and a lively Collingwood atmosphere.

What beer and drinks do you have?

We offer about 35 local and imported beers, plus draught, wine, cocktails, and Caesar Sunday specials. See the drinks section of our menu for the current list.

Hours, takeout & dining

What are Beaver & Bulldog’s hours?

We are open 7 days a week from 11am to 11pm.

Do you offer takeout in Collingwood?

Yes. Call 705-446-9711 for takeout. Regular menu pricing applies; Tuesday Wing Day specials are eat-in only.

Do you have a patio?

Yes. Outdoor patio seating is available in warmer weather — a popular choice for summer evenings in Collingwood.

Do you have a kids menu?

Yes. Our kids menu is for ages 12 and under and includes a fountain drink, entrée, and ice cream for $11.

Do I need a reservation?

Walk-ins are welcome. Evenings between about 5 and 7:30 can be busy, so allow a little extra time or call 705-446-9711 if you have a large group.

Where can I see the full menu?

View our full menu online, download the PDF, or ask for a menu when you arrive. The kids menu is here.

Location & South Georgian Bay

Where is Beaver & Bulldog located?

Beaver & Bulldog Sports Pub & Wingery is at 195 First St, Collingwood, ON L9Y 1A6. Call 705-446-9711 for directions or takeout.

Is Beaver & Bulldog close to The Blue Mountains?

Yes. We are in Collingwood on First Street, a short drive from Blue Mountain Resort and The Blue Mountains. Skiers, golfers, and weekend visitors often stop in for wings, burgers, and cold beer after a day on the hill.

Is Beaver & Bulldog a good stop from Wasaga Beach?

Yes. Guests from Wasaga Beach regularly drive into Collingwood for our jumbo wings, sports pub atmosphere, and patio. We are an easy trip along the South Georgian Bay corridor.

What towns and areas do you serve?

We welcome locals and visitors from Collingwood, The Blue Mountains, Wasaga Beach, Thornbury, Stayner, Clearview, Creemore, Nottawa, and across South Georgian Bay / Grey-Simcoe.

Is Beaver & Bulldog good for families visiting Collingwood?

Yes. Families visiting Collingwood, Blue Mountains, and Wasaga Beach enjoy our full pub menu, kids menu for ages 12 and under, and casual sports-pub setting.

Awards & social

Have you won any local awards?
