The Pub

Much like fur trading created the birth of Canada as a Nation; the Pub has come to clearly define what Britain is to the World. In the evolution of the Pub, it is now the Beaver & Bulldog that rewrites history and sets a new culinary course in dining and social gathering. Bringing together a truly inspired menu and a dash of local colour in an innovative Sports Pub & Wingery by the Pub Proprietor, Brandon Biggs.

Passion

Our quintessential Canadian Beaver has blended recipes and designs with our loyal British Bulldog together in a splendid selection of signature flavours and Canadian favorites, infused with British flair. Take special note of our world famous fresh jumbo buffalo wings, fresh fish, Canadian beef and our many local fresh market selections, all of which are seasoned to perfection by our talented Kitchen team that each add a personal touch and an abundance of passion.
