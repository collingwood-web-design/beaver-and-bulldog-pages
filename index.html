The Pub

Great wings, cold beer, live sports and good company. Welcome to Beaver & Bulldog, Collingwood’s sports pub and wingery, where Canadian favourites meet a little British flair.

As the fur trade helped shape Canada and the pub shaped Britain, Beaver & Bulldog brings both together — an inspired menu, a dash of local colour, and Collingwood’s own sports pub & wingery, from proprietor Brandon Biggs.

Best Wings in Collingwood

Our quintessential Canadian Beaver has blended recipes and designs with our loyal British Bulldog together in a splendid selection of signature flavours and Canadian favorites, infused with British flair. Take special note of our world famous fresh jumbo buffalo wings — 25 flavours that keep locals and visitors from Blue Mountains and Wasaga Beach coming back — plus fresh fish, Canadian beef and our many local fresh market selections, all seasoned to perfection by our talented Kitchen team.
